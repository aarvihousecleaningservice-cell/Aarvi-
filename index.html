Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our home deep cleaning services in Hyderabad.

How much does home deep cleaning cost in Hyderabad?

Our charges depend on the flat configuration (1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK or independent villa) and whether the house is furnished or vacant. Because Aarvi House Cleaning Services provides direct localized services in LB Nagar, Vanasthalipuram and Turkayamjal without middleman markups, you receive the most competitive rates. Call us at 732840213 for an instant customized estimate!

Do I need to provide any cleaning supplies or machines?

No! Aarvi House Cleaning arrives fully equipped with single-disc floor scrubbing machines, wet & dry industrial vacuums, ladders, micro-fiber dusters, squeegees, and specialized eco-safe tile & degreasing chemicals. We only require running water and an electrical connection at your premises.

Can you remove tough bathroom hard water stains?

Yes! Hyderabad borewell water frequently causes severe calcium and salt scaling on tiles, taps, and glass partitions. We use specialized non-corrosive descaling agents to safely eliminate scaling without eroding tile enamel or harming metallic chromium plating.

How much time does deep cleaning take for a 2BHK or 3BHK flat?

A standard 2BHK deep cleaning typically requires 4 to 5 hours, while a 3BHK or villa takes roughly 5 to 7 hours. We deploy a team of 3 to 5 trained professionals depending on your home’s area to ensure single-day completion.
